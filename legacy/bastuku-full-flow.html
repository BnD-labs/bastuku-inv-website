How it works

How to move with Bastuku

1.Request

Tell us what you're moving and where — house, office, or cross-border — and get an instant estimate right on the site, no waiting required.

Once you're happy with the range, confirm your date and we lock in your slot.

2.Pack & Move

Our team arrives on schedule to pack, load, and transport your belongings. You'll have a direct contact for the whole move — no chasing updates.

Need something adjusted on the day? Just tell your move lead, they'll sort it on the spot.

3.Unpack & Confirm

We place everything where you want it and do a walkthrough together before we leave — so you can confirm everything arrived in the same shape it left in.

Payment is only finalized once you're satisfied. No surprises at the end.
